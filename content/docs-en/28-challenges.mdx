# Practice Challenges

In every chapter you practiced exactly what that chapter taught, like driving lessons with an instructor beside you. The challenges here are more like driving on your own: nobody tells you which chapter to take the command from, and sometimes you need to combine several chapters.

There are 12 challenges, four per level. Each one has a task, a hint and a full solution with an explanation.

## How to work with the challenges

<Steps>

### Try on your own

Read the task and try to solve it without help. Stuck? `man` and `--help` (chapter 8) are part of the game.

### Open the hint

The hint points you in the right direction, without giving away the answer.

### Compare with the solution

Even if you solved it, peek at the solution. There's usually more than one way, and the explanation shows why the solution works.

</Steps>

The beginner challenges can be solved right here on the page, in the simulator: click "Solve it in the simulator", and the tasks check themselves off as you succeed. The rest are solved in a real terminal (chapter 3).

<Callout type="tip" title="A practice directory">
Before the intermediate and advanced challenges, create a separate directory for practice: `mkdir ~/practice && cd ~/practice`. That way none of your real files get hurt by mistake.
</Callout>

The "Solved!" marks are saved only in this browser, and don't count toward your course progress.
